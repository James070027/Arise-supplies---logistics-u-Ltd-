About Us - Wandegeya Opposite The Junction

One Freezer, One Promise

In 2019 we started with one deep freezer at Wandegeya, next to the mosque. Chefs kept telling us the same things: late deliveries, dishonest weight, old meat sold as fresh. We made three promises and kept them: fresh meat daily, honest weight, six AM delivery. We woke at 4AM to cut, pack and deliver, sometimes by boda when the van broke down. One customer told another. Today we serve over 500 hotels, restaurants, schools, supermarkets, butcheries and homes across Kampala, Wakiso, Entebbe and Mukono, with freezer trucks, food-grade bags and calibrated scales.

Mission, Vision, Values

Mission: Fresh, halal, affordable, accurately weighed meat delivered on time, every time.
Vision: East Africa's most trusted meat supply chain, farm to plate.
Values: Freshness, honesty (1KG means 1KG), halal integrity, punctuality, customer first, loyalty.

How to Pay

Cash on Delivery - Up to 500k

Orders of 500k or less can be paid in cash. The rider shows the weight on the scale, you pay, you get a receipt. Please prepare small change.

Orders above 500k must be paid digitally. Dial *165*3#, choose Send Money to 0766791805 (ARISE SUPPLIES AND LOGISTICS (U) LTD), enter amount and PIN, then send the confirmation SMS screenshot on WhatsApp. We dispatch once confirmed.
